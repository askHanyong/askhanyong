▸ What does a DP Coordinator actually do?

The DP Coordinator runs the IB Diploma Programme at the school. Every IB World School offering the Diploma is required to have one — this is the person who ensures the programme meets IB standards from the first day of Year 1 through to the final exams.

In practice, this means: registering every student for their IB exams, overseeing the Extended Essay, Theory of Knowledge, and CAS, keeping the school compliant with IB regulations, supporting teachers on marking requirements and syllabus changes, and advocating for students in special circumstances — illness, learning needs, family emergencies.

Think of the DP Coordinator as the bridge between the school, the students, the teachers, and the IB organisation itself. A good coordinator understands the full picture of what a student needs to complete the Diploma — not just one subject, but everything.
