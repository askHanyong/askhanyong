How we measure
Transparent, reproducible evaluation

Every accuracy score you see here is the result of a fully automated, unbiased evaluation pipeline — not self-reported or hand-picked. The process runs against real IB past paper questions with verified gold-standard answers.

Because the same rubric runs on every new batch of questions, accuracy is tracked over time — you can see if HAN is improving.

Step 1
HAN answers the question
The same Claude model used in production responds to a real IB past paper question, unprompted.
Step 2
Judge scores the response
A separate LLM judge compares HAN's response to the official IB gold answer and key steps, scoring 5 dimensions (0–8 total).
Step 3
Results are published here
All scores — including low-scoring questions — are included. No cherry-picking. Updated as new questions are evaluated.
